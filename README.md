Syudad La Realidad – Gamemode Description

The foundation of this gamemode originally comes from Wicked Gaming, a leaked script by Kiddoph that gained popularity in the SA-MP community. Instead of leaving it as it was, I decided to take the base, rework it, and upgrade it to meet modern standards. The project evolved into something far greater: a fully customized open.mp gamemode designed for medium roleplay servers.

One of the most significant upgrades is the inventory system. The old Wicked Gaming inventory was limited and outdated, so I rebuilt it into a FiveM-style inventory, giving players a more immersive and user-friendly way to manage their items. This change alone improved the roleplay experience, making it more dynamic and closer to modern RP standards.

On top of the inventory upgrade, I implemented new systems and commands that were not part of the original Wicked Gaming release. These include advanced administrative tools, custom roleplay features, and additional mechanics designed to keep gameplay balanced, engaging, and fair for everyone. The anti-cheat measures, MySQL integration, and Discord logging were also improved to ensure smooth performance and stable server management.

The name was also changed to give the gamemode its own identity. From Wicked Gaming, it is now called Syudad La Realidad (City of Reality). The vision of this project is to create a medium roleplay environment where players can immerse themselves in character development, storytelling, and interactive gameplay within the San Andreas Multiplayer world.

Syudad La Realidad is not just a rebrand; it is a rebirth of a once-leaked gamemode that has been modernized and transformed into something unique. With upgraded systems, fresh commands, and improved gameplay mechanics, it now stands as a new roleplay experience for the community.
